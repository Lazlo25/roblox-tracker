PillarMonetization Efficiency: earning a high position with modest CCU (Monetization Score ranks position × CCU, lower is better).

Key observations today

  • Efficiency is position x CCU (lower is better). In the top 30, Blade Ball stands out at #8 with only 17K CCU (16th most efficient of 497), followed by Gakuran (#13, 11K CCU), Kaiju Alpha (#22, 9K CCU at 51 days old), Welcome to Bloxburg (#27, 16K) and Tower Defense Simulator (#30, 10K).
  • Scale can buy the top spot without per-player efficiency: Steal An Egg is #1 but ranks 325th of 497 on efficiency, and Brookhaven (447th), Murder Mystery 2 (426th) and 99 Nights in the Forest (427th) sit near the bottom.
  • Donation hubs dominate the low-CCU end (Donation Central #231 with 241 CCU, Summer Donations, Simple DONATIONS, Forest Donations): the Social genre is the most efficient group (14 games, r=+0.47).
  • Efficiency rises with age: games 42 days old or younger sit at a median 22nd percentile versus 57th for games over a year old. The day's biggest climbers were all young (Survive a Million Monkeys +201 at 58 days, Anime Zero +154, dogs +148, Steal Animal Egg +146), which suggests the earning position lags behind player counts.
  • RPGs (37 games, r=+0.32), tower defense (r=+0.34) and more original concepts (r=+0.32 among 79 profiled games) earn more for their CCU; pet and egg games (r=-0.22), anime-themed games (r=-0.31) and '+1' titles (r=-0.28) earn less.

Titles, thumbnails, videos and descriptions

  • Title keywords tied to efficiency: 'Defense' r=+0.30. Tied to inefficiency: 'Egg' r=-0.50, 'RP' r=-0.43, 'Steal' r=-0.42, 'Anime' r=-0.33, '+1' r=-0.28.
  • Live-ops signals go with efficiency: bracket tags in titles ([UPD], [🎃], [SEASON]; 276 of 497 games, r=+0.20, same direction on all 3 days), event tags (r=+0.21) and redeem codes in descriptions (r=+0.20).
  • Pricing: games whose median developer product costs more than 79 Robux are more efficient (r=+0.29), and a premium pass above 999 Robux helps slightly (r=+0.14). Cheap 5-49 Robux impulse ladders, typical of template simulators, go with lower efficiency.
  • Servers with more than 24 players go with efficiency (r=+0.23); social spaces give status purchases (donations, cosmetics, admin items) an audience.
  • The efficient leaders monetize in different ways: Blade Ball sells themed sword rolls (37-75 Robux) and mode tickets, Gakuran sells 3-10 Robux character rerolls, Tower Defense Simulator sells premium towers up to 7,999 Robux, and Kaiju Alpha sells 650-749 Robux monster skins with no passes.
  • Thumbnail style shows no measurable link to efficiency yet (reward-flex r=+0.02, 79 profiled games); the efficient leaders mostly use character art.

Notable outliers

  • Korea Army: The most efficient game by the metric (#487 with 115 CCU), but its audience is likely Korean and asleep at the 3 PM ET snapshot (about 4 AM in Seoul). Time zones can flatter regional games.
  • Makeup Mania! BETA [LIMITED NECKLACE]: The least efficient game (#468 with 17K CCU) at 12 days old: a fresh breakout whose revenue hasn't caught up yet. Watch whether it climbs.
  • Huss Valley: #38 with 94K CCU at 37 days (481st of 497 on efficiency): the month's biggest breakout earns little for its size, consistent with the age lag.
  • Fish It!: #80 with 71K CCU at 716 days old: age alone doesn't guarantee monetization.

Actionable takeaways

  • Tends to · 50%Original concepts with depth (RPG progression, tower defense, competitive cosmetics) tend to earn more per player than template simulators; a template game needs a progression layer worth paying for.
  • May · 45%Price most developer products above 79 Robux and offer at least one premium item above 999 Robux; cheap impulse ladders alone may cap revenue per player.
  • Tends to · 50%Keep live operations visible: update and event tags in the title and redeem codes in the description tend to go with better efficiency.
  • May · 45%Monetization may lag popularity by weeks, so judge a new game's earning power after about day 43, not in its first month.
  • Tends to · 60%Treat efficiency extremes below about 1,000 CCU with caution: donation hubs and regional games dominate there, and one daily CCU snapshot can mislead.
